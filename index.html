Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Clivo ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile application Clivo.

By using Clivo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Photos: Images you upload to generate listings. These are sent to our servers for AI processing and are not stored permanently.
  • Device Information: We generate a unique anonymous user ID stored locally on your device to manage your credit balance.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the app, such as the number of posts generated.
  • Purchase Information: If you purchase credits, payment is processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not store payment details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI-powered product listings from your photos
  • To manage your credit balance
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To improve our AI models and app performance
  • To show relevant advertisements (via Google AdMob)

4. Photos and AI Processing

Photos you upload are sent to our secure backend server and to Anthropic's Claude AI API for analysis and listing generation. Photos are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored on our servers after processing is complete.

We do not use your photos to train AI models.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API: For AI-powered listing generation. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: For managing in-app purchases. Privacy Policy
  • Render.com: For hosting our backend servers. Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

We retain your anonymous user ID and credit balance locally on your device. Server-side logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days for security and debugging purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Clivo is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to delete your personal data
  • The right to opt out of advertising
  • The right to data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of Clivo after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
