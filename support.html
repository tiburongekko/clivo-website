📱 How to use Clivo

How do I generate a listing?

Simply upload photos of your item, select the category and who it's for, fill in optional details like brand and price, then tap "Generate post". The AI will create a complete Vinted listing in seconds.

How does the measurement reading work?

Place a measuring tape next to your item when taking photos. Our AI will automatically detect and read the measurements from the image.

Which languages are supported?

Clivo supports 9 languages: Hungarian, English, German, Polish, Romanian, Slovak, Czech, Bulgarian, and Greek. You can choose the post language independently from your phone language.

💳 Credits & Purchases

How many free credits do I get?

Every new user gets 3 free credits. Each listing generation uses 1 credit.

How can I get more credits?

You can watch a short video ad to earn 1 free credit, or purchase credit packages: Starter (30 credits), Pro (100 credits), or Power (300 credits).

How do I restore my purchases?

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, your purchases can be restored through the shop screen. Make sure you're using the same Apple ID or Google account.

🔧 Technical Issues

The app can't process my photo

Make sure your photos are in JPEG or PNG format. HEIC photos from iPhone are automatically converted. If the problem persists, try taking a new photo with the camera instead of uploading from gallery.

The generated listing is in the wrong language

You can change the post language in the "Post language" section before generating. This is independent from your phone's language setting.

The app shows a network error

Please check your internet connection and try again. The app requires an active internet connection to generate listings.
